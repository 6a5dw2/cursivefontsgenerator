What is in each PDF

Every download is a single three-page PDF, sized for US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and printing correctly on A4 when the printer is set to fit the page.

  • Page 1 — uppercase A–Z, with a baseline and a dotted midline guide.
  • Page 2 — lowercase a–z, same layout.
  • Page 3 — the full alphabet in light grey, so a pen or pencil line shows up over the top.

The letters are drawn with the actual font files this site uses for its signature tool — the same shapes you see in the previews above.

How to print and use them

  1. Open the PDF and choose Print. Set scale to 100% (not "fit to printable area") so the letters keep their intended size.
  2. Print on ordinary paper for tracing, or on card stock to use the first page as a desk reference.
  3. Trace the grey page with a pencil first, then try the dark pages freehand on a separate sheet.

Printing on A4

The sheets are laid out on US Letter. On A4 paper, select "Fit" in the print dialog; the layout shrinks by about 3% and the letters stay perfectly usable.

A note on what these are. These are letterform references drawn with decorative script fonts, not a handwriting curriculum. They show the shapes each style uses, but they do not teach stroke order, joins, or the letterforms taught in school handwriting programmes.

Frequently asked questions

Are these cursive worksheets free?

Yes. All four PDFs are free to download and print, with no signup, watermark, or page limit.

Can I print them on A4 paper?

Yes. Choose "Fit" in your print dialog and the US Letter layout scales down to A4 without cutting anything off.

Do these teach handwriting?

No. They are letterform references and tracing aids made with decorative script fonts. They do not show stroke order or the letter shapes used in school cursive programmes.

Can I use them in my classroom or share them with students?

Yes, print as many as you need. For a website or resource list, please link to this page instead of re-uploading the PDFs.
