Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how Cursive Font Generator uses information when you visit cursivefontsgenerator.com and use our tools.

Text you enter into our tools

Our tools process the text locally in your browser rather than sending it to our server. Text conversion and signature image generation happen on your device. When you copy a result or download a signature, it is saved to your clipboard or device at your request.

Microsoft Clarity analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this website and to improve its usability. Clarity provides behavioral analytics, heatmaps and session replay based on information such as page views, clicks, scrolling and browser or device details.

We do not intentionally use Clarity to collect the text you enter into our tools. Clarity applies masking to input fields. This does not mean the website collects no data: usage analytics are processed separately from the tools' local text conversion.

For details about Microsoft's data practices, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity FAQ.

Cookies and similar technologies

Microsoft Clarity may use cookies and similar technologies to provide analytics and session insights. These include cookies such as _clck and _clsk.

  • _clck retains a Clarity user identifier and preferences for this site.
  • _clsk associates page views with a Clarity session.

These examples are not an exhaustive cookie list. Microsoft may also use other cookies as described in its Clarity cookie documentation. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Regional consent and your choices

Microsoft requires a valid consent signal for Clarity cookie use in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Without analytics consent, Clarity may process limited cookieless data and some features may be unavailable. See Microsoft's consent documentation for details.

This policy explains our data practices; it is not a consent request. Reading this page or using a tool does not itself provide a consent signal.

What our tools do not require

You do not need an account or an email address to use the tools. We do not provide a server-side history of the text you enter or the signatures you generate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our tools or data practices change. The date above indicates the latest update.
